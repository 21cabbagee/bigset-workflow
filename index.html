Why this works

Funded companies have two things at once

💰

Budget

They just raised — they have money to spend and problems they're actively solving. That makes them the best leads if you sell.

🚀

Momentum

They're about to hire engineers — often before the job posting goes live. Apply before the crowd even knows.

Market Signal

What's getting funded is a real-time signal of where the market is moving. Use it to decide what to build or learn.

The workflow

Three steps from prompt to pipeline

Step 1

Type one sentence

Open Bigset and describe the dataset in plain English. No scrapers, no selectors, no config files. That sentence is the entire input.

Step 2

Agents build it from the live web

Bigset infers the schema automatically — columns, types, primary key. Then it sends a swarm of agents onto the live web.

  • 🎯
    Orchestrator agent does broad discovery and finds the companies
  • ⚡
    Sub-agents fan out in parallel, each scoped to a single company
  • 🔗
    Every cell is traceable back to its source — no hallucinations
  • 🔄
    Rows are deduplicated on a primary key
